## Sponsor the team

We are always looking to make new connections and build partnerships. If you or your organisation would like to sponsor the team, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Our current sponsors are on the [sponsors page](/sponsors/).

## Mentor

If you have experience in engineering, mechanics, programming or media and would like to mentor high school students, we would like to hear from you. Email us or use the [contact page](/contact/).

## Volunteer

We run WARP (the West Australian Robotics Playoffs), our offseason event at Curtin, and help run *FIRST* LEGO League and *FIRST* Tech Challenge events in WA. These events need volunteers. [Get in touch](/contact/) if you would like to help.
