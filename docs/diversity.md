We envision a Western Australia where every student, regardless of geography, identity, or socioeconomic background, has the opportunity to engage in meaningful STEM experiences.
{: .lead}

<div class="prose" markdown="1">

## What we do

- WARP, our offseason event, includes a Women+ in STEM event each year: a breakfast with guest speakers in 2025 and a dinner in 2026.
- Our 2026 preseason program offered an all-girls team alongside the mixed teams.
- Through WARP we help new teams get started at schools around WA, including lending a kit robot to get a new team driving.
- No experience is needed to join. New students learn alongside returning students and mentors.

If you have a question or an idea for how we can do better, [contact us](/contact/).
